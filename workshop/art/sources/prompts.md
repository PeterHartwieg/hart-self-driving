# Image prompts for HART. Self Driving (Steam Workshop)

Style for every image: match the supplied Big Ambitions game screenshot (reference only, never copy it): a clean, slightly stylised 3D city of New York, Unity HDRP look, soft realistic lighting, readable shapes, saturated but not neon. The cars are the game's kind of cars: rounded, simplified real-world shapes without badges. No text, no lettering, no logos, no number plates with text, no UI, no watermark, no glow effects, no lens flares.

## 1. hero-wide.png (landscape 16:9, at least 1920x1080)
A yellow New York taxi drives along a wide Manhattan avenue at early dusk, seen from a high three-quarter view behind and above, about 20 m up, so the road ahead and the next junction are visible. Street lights and shop windows are coming on, the sky above the towers is warm orange fading to deep blue. Some traffic cars further ahead wait at a red light; the taxi is the clear subject in the right half of the frame, moving towards the upper right. Keep the left 40 percent of the picture calm and darker (building shade, empty pavement), because a logo goes there later. The road in front of the taxi is empty for about 30 m, because a route line is drawn on it later.

## 2. hero-square.png (square, at least 1024x1024)
Same scene and lighting as image 1, recomposed for a square: the taxi lower centre, heading up the avenue towards a junction with a traffic light, the towers framing it. Keep the top 30 percent calm (sky and tower tops), because a logo goes there later. Keep the empty road ahead of the taxi.

## 3. taxi.png (transparent cutout, at least 1536x1024)
A yellow New York taxi sedan alone, front three-quarter view from slightly above, wheels turned a little as if driving. Soft studio light from upper left, a soft contact shadow under it is fine. Genuinely transparent background (alpha 0 around the car and in all four corners).

## 4. limo.png (transparent cutout, at least 1536x1024)
A black stretch limousine, the private driver's car, same view, light and transparency as image 3.

## 5. own-car.png (transparent cutout, at least 1536x1024)
A modern compact family hatchback in deep green (close to #00703A), same view, light and transparency as image 3. It stands for "your own car".
