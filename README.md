# Driven Rides

A mod for Big Ambitions. Taxi and private-driver rides drive through Manhattan instead of fading to black: you ride along, the camera follows the car, and you arrive at the door. A ride costs the same game time and money as in the base game, and **Skip** finishes it the base-game way at any moment.

Press **L** to call your private driver. Press **L** again once it waits, and you walk to it and choose where to go.

Status: in development (Milestone 0, the spike). Not on the Workshop yet.

## Options

In the game's Mods options: driven rides on or off, ride speed (1×, 2× or 3×; default 3×), and the driver shortcut on or off.

## Debug keys

Ctrl+Alt+Shift with: **G** graph stats, **X** clear a stuck ride, **K** remove the ride car, **V** hospital mid-ride, **U** throw inside the ride, **J** fake a save change. Spike builds only.
